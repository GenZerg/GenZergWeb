# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators, streamers, and content makers arriving to understand GenZerg. They are scanning for voice and personality, not filling out a workflow.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors discover who GenZerg is and why they should care. Success is when they leave remembering the story and vibe—not a feature list.

## Positioning

A living showcase of GenZerg’s work, voice, and vibe.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Explore the story / about; fallback Follow on social.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: There’s a story here worth exploring.
- Belief ladder: Feels playful and alive → There’s craft behind the vibe → I want to know who this is.
- Proof on hand: Social proof (followers, clips, streams) to be added later—no testimonials or logos yet.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bold, curious. The interface should feel delightful and playful—kinetic like a game brand, never stiff or corporate.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS: purple gradients, Inter, identical card grids.

## Design Principles

- **Story before features.** Every surface should make someone want to know who GenZerg is.
- **Play with craft.** Delight is earned through sharp execution, not noise.
- **Showcase, don’t sell.** Present work, voice, and vibe as a living exhibit.
- **Creator peer, not pitch deck.** Speak to creators as equals who get the culture.
- **Memorable in ten seconds.** Leave one clear impression: there’s a story worth exploring.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Respect reduced motion, maintain strong contrast, and keep focus states and alt text solid from the start.
